1. Main Flow: Complete Learning-Resolution Loop (PROVISIONALLY_RESOLVED)

2. Exception Flow: Explain-Back Clarification Retry & Persistent Gap Escalation

Illustrates the exception handling and escalation path when an explanation requires clarification (triggering a targeted hint and single retry) followed by a failed parallel question resulting in a PERSISTENT_GAP and tutor escalation.

Exception Path: Retry & Persistent Gap Escalation
