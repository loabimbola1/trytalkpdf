1. Screen Architecture & State Navigation Map

To preserve data bundles on slow 2G/3G connections and eliminate cognitive overload, the flow is consolidated into exactly 8 focused screens:

stateDiagram-v2 [*] --> SCR_01: Student Enters App SCR_01 --> SCR_02: Start 5-Question Quiz SCR_02 --> SCR_03: Incorrect Option Selected (Gap Diagnosed) SCR_03 --> SCR_04: I Understand (Ready to Explain) SCR_04 --> SCR_04: Needs Clarification (1 Retry Loop) SCR_04 --> SCR_05: Understood / Retry Complete SCR_05 --> SCR_06: Submit Reassessment Answer SCR_06 --> SCR_01: Practice Next Topic SCR_06 --> SCR_07: Automated WhatsApp Trigger SCR_07 --> SCR_08: Parent Taps Secure Link

2. Detailed Screen Specifications (SCR-01 to SCR-08)

Topic Selection & Diagnostic Setup
SCR-01
Purpose Lets the student select their target exam, subject, and specific syllabus objective to launch a 5-question diagnostic quiz.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Top Bar: TalkPDF logo, Student Name ("Samuel O."), and current mastery streak badge ("🔥 3 Gaps Closed").
  • 2 Data-Saver Indicator: "Low-Data Text Mode: Active" with a toggle switch (FR-08).
  • 3 Exam Selector Tabs: Two toggle buttons for WAEC / WASSCE and JAMB (UTME).
  • 4 Subject Dropdown Selector: Menu listing secondary subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics).
  • 5 Syllabus Topic Search & List: Search input followed by selectable syllabus topics with objective codes (e.g., PHY-EM-01: Electromagnetism — Faraday's Law).
  • 6 Launch Action Button: Full-width primary button labeled "Start 5-Question Diagnostic".
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping Exam Tabs: Filters the syllabus list to WAEC or JAMB specific objectives.
  • Tapping Subject Dropdown: Refreshes the topics list for the selected subject.
  • Tapping a Syllabus Topic: Highlights the target objective and activates the launch button.
  • Tapping Low-Data Toggle: Disables non-essential graphics for extreme bandwidth efficiency.
  • Tapping "Start 5-Question Diagnostic": Caches questions in local storage (FR-10) and transitions to SCR-02.
5-Question Diagnostic Quiz
SCR-02
Purpose Lets the student complete 5 curriculum-linked past-exam questions in a text-first interface to isolate conceptual learning gaps.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Progress Header: Question counter ("Question 2 of 5"), visual progress bar (40%), and topic badge ("Physics: Faraday's Law").
  • 2 Question Problem Card: Source citation ("WASSCE 2022 Q14"), problem text, and optional lightweight diagram.
  • 3 Option Selection List: Four vertical option cards (A, B, C, D) with clear typography.
  • 4 Navigation Footer: Secondary button ("Previous") and primary button ("Submit Answer" / "Next Question").
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping an Option Card: Selects that option with an accent cyan highlight border.
  • Tapping Next Question: Saves selection locally and transitions to the next question.
  • Tapping Submit Final Answer: Submits quiz. If any answer is incorrect, system isolates the pre-tagged misconception (FR-02) and transitions to SCR-03.
Likely Gap Diagnosis & Micro-Intervention
SCR-03
Purpose Shows the student their identified likely learning gap and delivers a concise 60–90 second plain-English conceptual lesson.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Diagnosis Alert Banner: Misconception icon and label: "Likely Learning Gap Identified" (probable, not definitive).
  • 2 Misconception Card: Shows question failed, option picked, and the underlying rule confused.
  • 3 Micro-Intervention Card (60–90s Read): 3-paragraph plain-English explanation, key formula box, and optional 45s audio snippet player (FR-03).
  • 4 Action Button: Sticky bottom button: "I Understand — Let Me Explain It Back".
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping Audio Player: Plays the bite-sized 45-second voice snippet for students with reading fatigue.
  • Tapping "I Understand — Let Me Explain It Back": Logs intervention completed and transitions directly to SCR-04.
Explain-Back Active Recall & Evaluation
SCR-04
Purpose Lets the student explain the concept in their own words, receive AI evaluation (UNDERSTOOD vs. NEEDS_CLARIFICATION), and access a targeted hint with one retry if needed.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Prompt Header: Challenge statement: "Explain Faraday's Law in your own simple words so an SS1 student could understand."
  • 2 Input Area: Multi-line text box (with live local storage auto-save indicator, FR-10) and microphone voice dictation button (FR-12).
  • 3 Primary Action CTA: Button labeled "Check My Explanation".
  • 4 Evaluation Feedback Panel (Appears post-submission): Shows primary badge (UNDERSTOOD or NEEDS_CLARIFICATION), targeted hint box (if clarification needed), and secondary rubric scores (Simplicity, Accuracy, Analogies, Composite /100, Badge).
  • 5 Progression Action: "Proceed to Verification Question" (if understood) OR "Try 1 Revision (Hint Applied)" (if clarification needed).
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Typing in Input Box: Persists text instantly to browser cache on each keystroke.
  • Tapping "Check My Explanation": AI evaluates against required understanding points (FR-05); displays Feedback Panel.
  • Tapping "Try 1 Revision": Opens retry input with hint pinned; after retry, advances to SCR-05.
  • Tapping "Proceed to Verification Question": Transitions directly to SCR-05.
Parallel Reassessment Question
SCR-05
Purpose Lets the student solve one fresh parallel past-exam question testing the identical learning objective to empirically verify gap closure.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Reassessment Header: Banner: "Final Verification: Prove You've Got It".
  • 2 Objective & Source Tag: Syllabus objective code and past-exam reference ("JAMB UTME 2021 Q28").
  • 3 Parallel Question Card: Fresh problem statement and diagram testing the exact rule.
  • 4 Option Cards (A, B, C, D): Four selectable answer choices.
  • 5 Verification Action Button: Primary button labeled "Submit & Verify Gap Closure".
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping an Option Card: Selects the option with highlight border.
  • Tapping "Submit & Verify Gap Closure": Validates answer against marking scheme, writes status to DB, and transitions to SCR-06.
Gap Resolution & Status Outcome
SCR-06
Purpose Displays the empirical learning-gap outcome (PROVISIONALLY_RESOLVED vs. PERSISTENT_GAP), updates mastery streaks, and provides next-step actions.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Status Hero Banner: Green celebration card with PROVISIONALLY_RESOLVED badge (if passed) OR Red alert card with PERSISTENT_GAP badge (if failed).
  • 2 Mastery Streak Box: Updated count ("🔥 4 Gaps Closed", FR-14) and note that future reassessment will confirm permanent mastery.
  • 3 Tutor Escalation Card (Only on PERSISTENT_GAP): Callout with button "Request Tutor Help for this Topic" (FR-13).
  • 4 Parent Notification Notice: Text: "A progress scorecard has been dispatched to your parent via WhatsApp."
  • 5 Next Topic CTA: Primary button labeled "Practice Next Topic".
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping "Request Tutor Help": Pre-attaches diagnostic record and dispatches escalation ticket to human tutor.
  • Tapping "Practice Next Topic": Resets session and navigates back to SCR-01.
Parent WhatsApp Progress Summary
SCR-07
Purpose Displays the structured progress message received by the parent directly in their WhatsApp app, providing verifiable proof of study and gap closure.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 WhatsApp Header: Contact banner: "TalkPDF Study Bot" with official verification badge.
  • 2 Incoming Message Bubble:
    • Header: "📊 TalkPDF Exam Readiness Alert"
    • Student & Subject: "Samuel Okon | WAEC Physics"
    • Topic: "Electromagnetism: Faraday's Law"
    • Key Metrics: Gaps Identified (1), Provisionally Resolved (1), Persistent Gaps (0), Readiness Index (62% ➔ 74%)
    • Secure Web Link: https://talkpdf.app/r/sec-9a7d8e21c4
  • 3 Reply Bar: Native WhatsApp input field for parent replies.
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping Secure Web Link: Opens mobile browser directly to SCR-08 with no username, password, or login required (FR-07).
Parent Detailed Web Evidence Report
SCR-08
Purpose Lets the parent view the full, read-only evidence report (questions failed, Explain-Back evaluation, parallel result, and tutor recommendations) via a secure, non-guessable link without logging in.
Elements on the Screen (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)
  • 1 Report Header: Verified badge, Candidate Name ("Samuel Okon"), Target Exam, and Date Generated.
  • 2 Privacy Security Notice: "Private, read-only link. No student personal data exposed."
  • 3 Readiness Gauge: Visual percentage progress dial showing readiness growth (62% to 74%).
  • 4 Audit Trail Timeline:
    • Step 1 Diagnostic: Failed question, student answer, and likely misconception.
    • Step 2 Explain-Back: Student's own explanation transcript & AI UNDERSTOOD rating.
    • Step 3 Reassessment: Parallel question empirical pass result.
  • 5 Tutor Action Guidance: Recommendation note advising whether paid tutoring is required or if the concept is mastered.
  • 6 Footer Actions: "Download 1-Page PDF Summary" and TalkPDF Academic Advisor WhatsApp button.
Element Interactions & Triggers
  • Tapping "Download 1-Page PDF Summary": Generates downloadable PDF certificate of progress.
  • Tapping Academic Advisor Button: Launches WhatsApp conversation with TalkPDF tutor support.
